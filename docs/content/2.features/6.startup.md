The **Startup Manager** shows programs that start when you sign in:

- the `Run` registry keys for your account and for all users (64-bit and 32-bit),
- the Startup folders for your account and for all users.

## Turning items on and off

Tuntas switches items the same way Task Manager's **Startup apps** tab does. The original entry is never deleted; Windows simply skips items marked as disabled. That means:

- a disabled item can always be enabled again, from Tuntas or from Task Manager,
- Task Manager and Tuntas always agree on what is enabled.

Items for **All users** need administrator rights to change.
